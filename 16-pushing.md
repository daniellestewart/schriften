# Pushing and Fixing

One of the responses that my Lord often has for me is: fix your eyes on Me. When I don't know what to do, when I am confused why things are what they are, He reminds me to trust Him.

I will write about two scenarios today. They are on my mind this morning. 

## Morningtime with Holy Spirit

I've always had a very specific morning routine. Throughout the years, it has morphed and adjusted based on many factors, but it is an important time for me. Within the couple of weeks of giving my life to Christ, I ran into a teaching by a prominant man of God who walks with Holy Spirit closely. He told stories of his morningtimes with Holy Spirit and how valuable and beautiful they are. He said that within 20 minutes, he feels the presence of Holy Spirit without fail. He pushes through the initial distractions, then His presence greets him. Every single day. 

*Oh, 20 minutes is easy!* I imagined laying in His presence every day and basking in His beauty. I started switching my routine. It has never been difficult for me to focus my attention, so 20 minutes went by. Then 30, 40. *Where is His presence? What did I do wrong? Ah, perhaps it takes a week or so to get into the groove with Him. Maybe He needs to see that I am serious and committed.* A couple of days went by and I couldn't help but feel that I was doing something wrong. Maybe music? Maybe the right verses? Then Holy Spirit spoke to me.

> You cannot summon Me.

Oh. Ouch. That was exactly what I was attempting... What spell ingredients was I missing? Was my intention pure? What must I do? All of these things were my attempts to summon Him so that I could feel His presence. After my sincere apology, He began to teach me how to build a relationship with Him in these morning hours. He encouraged me to be silent with Him - no music, no checklists, no expectations or plans. He began to guide my prayers, show me beautiful things, and answer my questions. He discusses scripture, dreams, and situations with me, and He meets me every day.

However, He does not meet me as I imagined He would. I wish His presence showed up in a cloud of glory every morning, but it does not. Occasionally, very occasionally, He does share His beautful heavy presence with me and those days are special indeed. However, almost every day, I don't feel Him like that. I just know He is with me. And my mornings are more valuable to me than I could describe.

I believe this is His way of building a relationship with me that is not just built on feeling good. Most mornings I have to fight the sleepiness, random thoughts, and just not wanting to push anymore. But I push regardless of what I feel, and He always meets me there.

## Fasting

I have fasted many times in my life for health reasons. I've fasted often enough that I know my body, how it handles lack of food, and how to break fasts well. I know that there are times of fasting that are brutally challenging and other times that go smoothly. I know the importance of salt and water and I understand how fasts change depending on my cycle.

Lately, I've been hearing a lot of people mention fasting in their teachings and preachings. They speak of it as an important, vital part of our walk with God. Over and over I hear them say that He gives us grace while fasting, that they hear Him more clearly, and that fasting seems to remove veils between us and the spiritual world.

I've also been feeling a growing pressure to fast and an understanding that the upcoming season in my life will have fasting in it.  So, in my usual way, I imagined what it would be like for me. I would push through the first few hours of being hungry, then His grace would flood in and it would be amazing. I'd hear Him even more clearly, His presence would surely come, and I'd have amazing experiences in prayer and closeness with Him!

I am smiling writing this. I am on the last day of a three day water & salt only fast and it has been anything but what I imagined. This is by far the most difficult fast I have ever done. My body and soul seem to be at war with my spirit this week. Fuzzy headed slowness in my thinking, emotionally down, occasional emotional tantrums, cry easily, get frustrated and irritated easily, lack of energy.

Then there is the spiritual side on top of this. Waves of thoughts (that are not from me or God), anxiety trying to make its way into my home, attacks of doubt and insecurity. And to top it all off, I heard my God's voice SO much better when I wasn't fasting.  It doesn't feel like veils have been removed between me and the spiritual world. It seems more like layers of cotton batting have wrapped around my head and I am constantly cutting it off. In moments of clarity after battles, He reminds me: fix your eyes on Me. He tells me: this is necessary right now, keep pushing.

The longer this fast goes, the more He is revealing to me that was in my heart, I just didn't know it. So between the blows from the enemy, cutting off batting, and telling my body to shush, I also have revelations that require repentence and a change of heart. This is really difficult. But I push on and I fight to keep my eyes on Him.  

## Conclusion

It is so easy to read snippets of what someone else experiences and imagine it for myself. This is good to do! Desire His gifts and let's all provoke one another to beautiful things. However, to simply read the experiences someone else has and not see the context and cost is to only look at the veins of gold and forget about the mine around it.

Just because my mornings weren't as someone else said they "should" be, did not stop me from pushing and giving my time to Him. Just because this fast is really frustratingly difficult doesn't mean that there isn't something important happening with it that will shape my fasts to come. And just because it looks different for someone else does not mean that I am doing it wrong. It may mean that our Father knows exactly what each of us needs in this moment, even better than what we think we need.

And because I know He is reading this too, I will say... more of Your beautiful weighty presence would be super welcome... just sayin'.
