# The Path

One recent evening, I knelt before our beautiful Creator and spoke with Him about the following verses.

John 5:19-20
> Jesus replied, "Truly I tell you, the Son is not able to do anything on His own, but only what He sees the Father doing. For whatever the Father does, the Son likewise does these things. For the Father loves the Son and shows Him everything He is doing, and He will show Him greater works than these so that you will be amazed..."

How can a person reach the point of only doing what they see the Father doing? It would require that they gave up their own will entirely. How do I do that? Father told me that was what Jesus did during those forty days and nights of fasting. That wasn't a single effort event, but He had built up to that fast over many years of small increments and steps. However, Jesus gave up His own will and authority those days in the wilderness and traded it for Father's will.

Holy Spirit reminded me of the verse:

Luke 13:24
> Make every effort to enter through the narrow door...

Make every effort. Do I make every effort? What kind of path lies ahead of me if I make every effort? What kind of path lies before me if I take those steps towards losing my will for the sake of the will of my Father?

He showed me.

Recall the mines of Moria in the Fellowship of the Ring, in particular, the Bridge of Khazad-dum: the narrow stone bridge where Gandalf faced the Balrog. This was what I remembered as He showed me a path that I could choose.

The path ahead of me was more narrow than what is reasonable for any path, but especially one with no rails, no handholds, and sheer dropoffs on both sides. Loose rocks scattered the path and crumbled edges made it impossible to traverse. One would have to know every safe step before they could ever cross it. I could not see the bottom of the drop-off and I could not see the end of the path as it curved out of sight. It was an impossible path.

I began to cry at the overwhelming weight and cost of taking such a path. No one could ever walk this path... it is impossible. Then Jesus spoke to me as He stood on that path, "By yourself, it is impossible, but with Me all things are possible. I have already walked this path and I know the steps you will need to take. I lead, you follow."

I knew that the only way to ever walk this path was to lock my eyes onto His and not ever waiver. *Make every effort...* I also knew He was inviting me and I could choose this path, but it was not necessary - there were other narrow paths that were not quite so absurdly narrow. I looked back to my Father, where I sat looking out upon all of this. "Do you want me to walk this path?" I asked Him. He smiled softly and held up a scroll that rolled open.

On the scroll was a sketch of this pathway. I knew beyond any doubt that He drew this and the scroll told of my destiny. I bowed my head, "Then I will take this path. How do I take the first step?" He said softly, "You already have."
