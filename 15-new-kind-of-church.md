# A New Kind of Church: A Dream

The building was large, but not massive. It was built on the massive rocks at the edge of the endlessly stormy ocean. I walked into the front door. It was quiet in the building and had the warm glow of firelight. There were no artificial lights at all.

## The Entry
The front room was an entry and had plenty of places for hats, coats, and boots. There were small tables in front of the entry windows and on the tables were a few pots with green herbs growing. A rack for firewood sat next to the door. It was well stocked. The walls were rough sawn lumber, but it was fitting and comfortable. Some pretty knickknacks sat on windowsills and an umbrella stand stood in the corner.

Immediately I felt that the building was welcoming and peaceful. I knew that this entry was a place where people greeted each other fondly and stripped off their protective clothing, knowing they were now safe.

## The Inner Room
The inner room immediately off the entry was the kitchen area. It reminded me of a cantina. Some small tables, a bar area with herbs hanging in bundles. Some glass cups, big pots for making food, the smell of baking bread. It was a very comfortable place, dimly lit by firelight and lanterns.

There were wooden pillars throughout and all the walls were rough sawn boards. Despite their roughness, it created a really cozy feel and a solid construction. Little nooks were at the edges of the room where people could rest. The bar was right at the kitchen so that one could visit with the cook while they worked.

## The Sanctuary

The next room was the sanctuary. The sanctuary was still and silent. No one was in it at the moment. The walls were smooth wood, all stained very very dark. The wood looked like oak. The floor matched. Lanterns hung on the walls, spaced evenly apart. The fire glowed in this space.

Tables were placed around the large room. Some tables were ovals and some were shaped like a kidney bean. A candle sat at the center of each table. This is where we would grow close in intimacy and share the bread.

This room was so still that it forced one to walk gently and keep even your thoughts at a whisper. It was beautiful. 


## Thoughts about the Dream

Even as I write, I feel the warmth of that place stir within my spirit. The same fire that lit those rooms is burning in me. In you. Do you feel it too? We are the temple of the Holy Spirit. What do your rooms look like?

At first, I am so tempted to look at it as a blueprint for a future church building. How lovely such a church would be! Each meeting would be so different than the American church routine we know well. Then, it slowly dawns on me that I am that building. The relationship that His people have with one another is that building. The church, His bride, is growing and transforming into that building.

>Matt 7:24-25
>
> Therefore, anyone who hears these words of mine and acts on them will be like a wise man who build his house on the rock. The rain fell, the rivers rose, and the winds blew and pounded that house. Yet it didn't collapse, because its foundation was on the rock.

We walk through the valley of the shadow of death every day in this life, and the ocean of this world is ever stormy. We must build our house on Jesus Christ, the Rock of our salvation.

> Psalm 62:1 (honestly, all of Psalms 61-62)
>
> I am at rest in God alone; my salvation comes from Him. He alone is my rock and my salvation, my stronghold; I will never be shaken.

When we do, our life will be a place of peace that the world will not understand. Those who enter our doors will find rest. Herbs of healing will greet them and they will be able to let down their guard and remove the layers they pile upon themselves to survive the winds of the world.

Recall the one thing that was directly by the front door? The firewood box. Just as the virgins prepared oil for their lanterns, so must we prepare fuel for the Holy Spirit fire that will warm ourselves and all who enter our space. The firebox must not go empty. We have to have fuel for this fire. It is what brings light, warmth, and heat for the oven. His fire must be our only light source.

The kitchen was a place of deeper healing and preparation of the bread and meat that would sustain us. It was a place of rest and companionship. I must ask myself, who prepares our daily bread?

> John 6:35
>
> "I am the bread of life," Jesus told them. "No one who comes to Me will ever be hungry and no one who believes in Me will ever be thirsty again."

> John 6:51
>
> I am the living bread that comes down from heaven. If anyone eats of this bread he will live forever. The bread that I will give for the life of the world is My flesh... Truly I tell you, unless you eat the flesh of the Son of Man and drink His blood, you do not have life in yourselves. The one who eats My flesh and drinks My blood has eternal life and I will raise Him up on the last day, because My flesh is true food and My blood is true drink. The one who eats My flesh and drinks My blood remains in Me, and I in Him. Just as the living Father sent Me and I live because of the Father, so the one who feeds on Me will live because of Me. This is the bread that came down from heaven; it is not like the manna your ancestors ate - and they died. The one who eats this bread will live forever.

> Matt 6:9, 11
>
> Our Father in heaven...
> Give us this day our daily bread.

Jesus is our bread and nourishment. The Father gives Him to us. I believe that Holy Spirit is the One who prepares the meal, but He prepares us for it, not the other way around.

The sanctuary was the room of holiness and intimacy, both with each other and with our God. I believe there is a certain kind of intimacy that cannot be experienced without the fellowship of each other. The darkly stained wood was reminiscent of the blood stained cross that lined every single surface: wall, ceiling, floor, and table. The fire of Holy Spirit made that room glow.

There was no place for a stage or spotlight. The focus was on communion with each other. Every table was made to be close to one another, not to look at a single person. There were no icons, no decorations, no distractions. Just His fire, His presence, His holiness, and each other.

What would it be like to be in such a place?
Beautiful Holy Spirit, please prepare me to be this place. Prepare us.
